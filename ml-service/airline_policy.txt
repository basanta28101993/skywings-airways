SkyWings Airlines Official Policy Document

1. Cancellation Policy:
- Cancellations made more than 24 hours before departure receive a 100% refund.
- Cancellations made within 24 hours of departure incur a ₹3,000 penalty.
- Non-refundable tickets cannot be cancelled.

2. Baggage Policy:
- Economy class: 15kg check-in, 7kg cabin.
- Business class: 30kg check-in, 10kg cabin.
- Extra baggage can be purchased online for ₹500 per 5kg.

3. Date Change Policy:
- Date changes are allowed up to 4 hours before departure.
- A change fee of ₹2,000 plus fare difference applies.

4. Refund Timeline:
- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.
